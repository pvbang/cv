❓ CONTROLS GUIDE

Movement

WASD / arrows Walk

Mouse Look around

Space Jump / swim up

Space×2 Toggle flight (creative style)

Ctrl / Shift Sprint (FOV widens)

Shift / Q Descend while flying

Blocks

Left click Hold to mine (crack animation) · click an animal to hit it

Right click Place the block in hand, or open an info block

Middle click Pick the block you are looking at

1–9 / Wheel Select hotbar slot (1-4 tools: pickaxe, axe, shovel, sword)

Tab Open the inventory (tools + blocks)

Alt+Wheel Mid-air placement distance (only while holding a block)

The ghost preview appears only when you hold a block. Right tool = faster mining: pickaxe for stone/ore, axe for wood, shovel for dirt/sand.

The cyan "i" blocks are CV info points and cannot be broken.

CV exploration

E Talk to a nearby NPC / open an info block / close a panel

C CV Compendium (everything you unlocked)

M Big map and fast travel

H This controls guide

Esc Pause / close any window

Extras

F Fast-forward day and night

F2 Save a screenshot (PNG)

F3 Debug screen (coordinates, FPS, time)

V Zoom (hold)

🔊 Mute / unmute sound
