Eternal Odyssey – Unity 3D action RPG (team of two)

UnityC#NavMeshFirebase Realtime DBTextMesh Pro

  • Scope: 174 first-party C# files (about 10–13K lines) in 12 domain folders: characters, controllers, models (28 data classes and 19 enums), inventory, quests, realtime, systems and UI.
  • Online play: custom Firebase sync instead of a networking SDK: login and registration, player spawn and room logic, Lerp-interpolated remote players with animation-state sync, shared world objects and chest contents.
  • Combat and enemies: NavMesh patrol / chase / attack with sight and attack ranges and Animator state behaviours; enemy combo counter that picks from 7 skills with min/max damage and critical chance; player base attack, shield, projectile and area skills; a buff and debuff API on a rich attribute model (damage, health, speed, crit).
  • Systems: drag-and-drop inventory with item actions, ground items and chests; quest manager, quest world and quest UI; custom Editor inspectors for quest and weapon lists.
  • AI integration: in-game assistant posts player questions to a RAG API and prints the streamed answer character by character, with spoiler-aware persona prompts.
  • Design: ER diagram and use-case model (StarUML) plus a game-design document: 5 elemental classes, 3-tier weapons with proc effects and EXP rewards scaled by enemy tier.
